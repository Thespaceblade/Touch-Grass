01 How it works

Phones do the tagging.
You just run.

Touch Grass uses iPhone Bluetooth and GPS to track tags, zones, and roles in real time, so the rules enforce themselves and you can keep your eyes on the real world.

Step 1

Draw the bubble

Pan the map. The bubble is your play zone. Step outside it and you’re flagged. Shrink it for a tighter game.

Step 2

Tag by Bluetooth

Get close, get tagged. No buttons to mash, no honor system: proximity does the work.

Step 3

Heads-up HUD

Glanceable chips for time, zone, alive count, role and flags. Eyes stay up. Phones stay in pockets.

How it plays

  1. Auto-pick teams or set them in the lobby. Each team gets a flag player.
  2. Cross the field to grab the enemy flag and bring it back to your safe zone.
  3. Tagged? Drop the flag. Return your own to keep the score honest.

ZB Game Mode · 02

Zombie
Tag.

Patient zero is loose. Every tag turns a human. Outlast the timer or fall to the swarm.

Players
6–16
Length
5–10 min
Roles
2

How it plays

  1. One player starts infected. Everyone else is human. Bluetooth tracks who is near you.
  2. Tagged humans turn zombie instantly and join the hunt.
  3. Survive the timer to win. Last human standing earns the badge.
MH Game Mode · 03

Manhunt.

Hunters chase. Hiders run. The bubble keeps shrinking. Don’t get caught.

Players
4–12
Length
6–12 min
Zone
shrinks

How it plays

  1. Pick hunters and hiders in the lobby. Hunters get a 30-second head start to spread out.
  2. Hide, sprint, peek. The zone shrinks every few minutes, so no easy corners forever.
  3. Last hider standing wins the round. Hunters win by clearing the field.
